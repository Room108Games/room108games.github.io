THE CRISIS OF NAVROS // PRESIDENTIAL BRIEFING

You have just been elected as the President of Navros. The celebrations are short-lived: your own political party is aggressively pushing toward war with neighbouring Solonia.

The game is a multiple ending, multiple choice narrative that has a gritty undertone around difficult topics, accompanied by an evocative, original musical score. Every decree signed in the quiet of the executive office reverberates across the front lines, the capital plazas, and the history books.
